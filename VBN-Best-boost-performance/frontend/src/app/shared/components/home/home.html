Terms & Conditions

Please read and accept before continuing

30-Day Free Trial Policy

  • All users (Students, Teachers & Principals) are granted a 30-day free trial upon registration.
  • The trial is tracked by daily login count. Each unique day you log in counts as one trial day.
  • After 30 login days, access to the application will be restricted until payment is completed.
  • The admin may manually extend or reset your trial period at any time.
  • Teachers and Principals may be exempt from payment requirements as configured by the Admin.

Daily Quiz Rules

  • The Daily Quiz can be attempted only once per day. Once submitted, you cannot retake it on the same day.
  • A new quiz becomes available at 12:00 AM every day.
  • Skipping a day does not carry over — each day's quiz is independent.
  • Your daily quiz score is reflected immediately in the Leaderboard.

Question & Test Timing

  • Each question has a fixed time limit. Once the timer runs out, the question is automatically skipped.
  • The overall quiz has a total time limit based on the number of questions selected.
  • You cannot go back to a previous question once you have moved forward.
  • If the browser tab is closed or refreshed mid-quiz, the quiz session may be lost.
  • Ensure a stable internet connection before starting any quiz.

General Rules & Usage Policy

  • Your account is personal and must not be shared with others.
  • Any attempt to manipulate scores or the leaderboard may result in account suspension.
  • All data submitted is stored securely and used only for educational performance tracking.
  • The platform is intended for educational use within registered schools only.
